About Anand Library

Study with focus.
Prepare with confidence.

Started in August 2024, Anand Library was created with a simple idea — provide students with a clean, comfortable and focused environment where they can study without unnecessary distractions.

Located on Pipra Road in West Ramkrishna Nagar, Patna, our centre offers 102 individual study desks with privacy partitions, LED lighting, power sockets, air conditioning, and a peaceful atmosphere tailored for competitive exam aspirants.
